# FINCONTAGION-X

## 1. Product Overview

**Project name:** FINCONTAGION-X

**Full name:** Financial Contagion, Systemic Risk & Stress Intelligence Platform

### 1.1 Purpose

FINCONTAGION-X is an India-centred financial stress-testing and decision-support platform designed to simulate how financial shocks propagate through interconnected banks, non-banking financial companies (NBFCs), investment funds, corporations and suppliers.

The platform will combine quantitative financial modelling, network analysis, machine learning, scenario evaluation, financial-document retrieval and controlled AI workflows.

### 1.2 Core Problem

Financial institutions and businesses are connected through lending, investments, funding arrangements, shared asset holdings and payment obligations. A shock affecting one entity may therefore create losses or liquidity pressure elsewhere in the network.

The project aims to model these transmission mechanisms, identify potentially vulnerable entities, explain how stress propagates, and compare possible risk-mitigation interventions.

### 1.3 Primary Objectives

1. Construct an interconnected financial ecosystem using synthetic data and, where feasible, verified public data.
2. Model financial positions, exposures, cash flows, capital and liquidity.
3. Simulate interest-rate, asset-price, credit and funding shocks.
4. Trace contagion across financial institutions and connected corporations.
5. Measure network vulnerability and systemic importance.
6. Develop and evaluate machine-learning-based risk indicators.
7. Compare stress scenarios and potential interventions.
8. Retrieve evidence from financial documents and generate source-grounded explanations.
9. Provide an interactive analyst interface and a deployable application.

### 1.4 Scope and Limitations

The initial system is a research and decision-support simulator. Its outputs depend on the quality of the input data, modelling assumptions and implemented transmission mechanisms.

Synthetic data will be explicitly labelled and will not be presented as observed financial data. Predictive claims will require appropriate real-world validation.

The platform will distinguish modelled losses, liquidity outflows, asset revaluations and other financial effects to avoid double-counting.
